# Sir Gerald

![alt text](mdPaste/gerald/image.png)

## Lore

The guardian of justice, the punisher of darkness, the protector of the innocent, the smasher of evil, the seeker of light — Gerald is arguably the most extraordinary figure in the Linirean army!

## Skills

### Holy Support

Every 13 seconds the knight summons a rallyable Royal Guard to help in battle. A Royal Guard has 250 HP and 40 armor, deals 10-30 physical damage per attack, and stays for 14 seconds.

### Inspiration

Every 8.33 seconds, when at least 2 allies are nearby, Gerald strikes his shield to inspire them: they immediately restore 30% of their max HP and for the next 6 seconds gain 16 damage, 15 armor and 15 magic resistance. The resistance bonus and the healing are halved for heroes.

### Shield of Retribution

Each time Gerald takes a melee attack he has a 75% chance to raise his shield and counter, negating the damage and dealing 200% of it as true damage. Against BOSS units the chance is x50%; against area attacks it is x60%.

### Holy Strike

Every 8 seconds Gerald performs a holy strike, dealing 108-180 magic damage to enemies within range 55 and silencing them for 4 seconds.

## Review

A tough, solid melee hero with excellent base stats and fast leveling.

- Holy Support chains seamlessly and provides a high-quality blocker, giving the knight the ability to block several enemies.
- Inspiration gives him sustain and greatly improves the survivability and damage output of nearby soldiers, making him a solid barracks core.
- Shield of Retribution gives him extremely strong single-target blocking, with solid tankiness and considerable damage.
- Holy Strike rounds out his AoE, and its mass silence matters in some situations.

Usage tip: pair him with a barracks.
